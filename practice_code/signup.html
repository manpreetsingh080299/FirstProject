Academic Support

We provide assistance to our researchers throughout all stages of the research life cycle, from development of ideas, through preparation of competitive applications to management and delivery of funded research projects.

We also provide accurate costings, expert guidance, skills development, and advice in the use of our Research Management Administration System (RMAS). Some resources require you to log in to the staff website.
