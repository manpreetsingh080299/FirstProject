Acadmic Support

We provide assistance to our researchers throughout all stages of the research life cycle, from development of ideas, through preparation of competitive applications to management and delivery of funded research projects.

We can also provide accurate costings based on the FEC model and pricing to ensure effective cost recovery, expert guidance on the rules and regulations of research funders, skills development for researchers and continuous support, and advice in the use of our Research Management Administration System (RMAS). University academics will find a number of useful resources on this page. Please note that some of these will take you to other sections of our external website, as well as the staff website which will require you to login.
